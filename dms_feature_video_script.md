# DMS.app Feature Walkthrough — Video Script / Storyboard

Target length: ~4–5 minutes. Adjust pacing per scene as needed.

---

## 1. Cold open (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** DMS launcher opening, browser tab loading to the folder tree.
**Narration:** "This is DMS — a document management system built for engineering assemblies. Every drawing, photo, and file lives on your own hard drive, organized the way your team actually works."

## 2. Folder tree & organization (0:15–0:45)
**Visual:** Create a folder, rename it, drag it to reorder/move, show serial number field.
**Narration:** "Build a folder tree that mirrors your assembly structure. Drag to reorder or move folders — the real files move with them. Add serial numbers to keep everything numbered consistently."
**Also show:** Recycle bin — delete an item, open "Not Show in Tree" bin, restore it.

## 3. Getting files in (0:45–1:30)
**Visual:** Drag-and-drop a file; paste a screenshot with Cmd/Ctrl+V; show mobile upload QR code and a phone camera shot landing in the folder; batch ZIP import auto-sorting.
**Narration:** "Getting documents in is flexible: drag and drop, paste screenshots straight from your clipboard, or scan a QR code to snap a photo on your phone and have it land directly in the right folder. Need to bring in a whole batch at once? Drop in a ZIP and DMS sorts files into folders automatically."

## 4. Viewing & data extraction (1:30–2:10)
**Visual:** Open a PDF preview, open a scanned image showing OCR'd text, show key-parameter auto-extraction populating metadata fields.
**Narration:** "Every document previews right in the browser — PDFs, images, even HEIC and Office files. Scanned documents get OCR'd automatically, so their text is searchable. DMS can even pull key parameters — design values, actual values — straight out of a document into structured fields."

## 5. Search (2:10–2:30)
**Visual:** Type a search term, results filtering live across filenames, OCR text, metadata.
**Narration:** "Find anything instantly — by document number, filename, or content — across the entire library."

## 6. Face recognition (2:30–3:00)
**Visual:** Select photos, tag a face with a name, open the People list, click a person to see all their photos, auto-scan finding more matches.
**Narration:** "For photo-heavy projects, DMS can recognize faces. Tag someone once, and it builds a People list — click a name to see every photo they're in, or auto-scan a folder to find more."

## 7. Databook & merging (3:00–3:25)
**Visual:** Select multiple documents, generate a databook PDF with cover page and table of contents; merge a folder of scans into one PDF.
**Narration:** "When it's time to hand off a package, generate a branded databook — cover page, table of contents, bookmarks — from any set of documents. Or merge a folder of scans into a single PDF in one click."

## 8. Sharing & remote access (3:25–3:55)
**Visual:** Send to Phone flow — generate link, send via SMS/iMessage; Remote Upload from the launcher — QR code, someone uploading from their own phone.
**Narration:** "Need to show someone a document on-site? Send it to their phone with a link, no app required. Or flip it around — generate a public upload link so a colleague or contractor can upload files directly into your project from anywhere."

## 9. Sync, backup & security (3:55–4:25)
**Visual:** OneDrive folder picker; export .dms backup; CSV export; app password screen.
**Narration:** "Point your storage folder at OneDrive and everything backs up automatically. Export a full backup any time, or just the metadata as a CSV. And if you need to lock things down, set a password to protect the whole library — even on shared links."

## 10. Close (4:25–4:45)
**Visual:** Return to folder tree overview, DMS logo/title card.
**Narration:** "DMS — your documents, organized, searchable, and always under your control. Get started with the launcher on Mac or Windows today."

---

## Production notes
- Each scene = one screen-recorded clip; record at 1080p+, app window maximized, cursor visible.
- Consider a consistent zoom-in on the specific button/field being demonstrated (most editors support "highlight cursor" or zoom keyframes).
- Record narration separately (voiceover) rather than live audio — easier to re-take lines.
- Background music: low-volume, no lyrics, under the voiceover.
