无法打开？

Can't open it?

Mac：提示"Apple 无法验证...是否含有恶意软件"

  1. 看到该提示后点击"完成"（不要点"移到废纸篓"）。
  2. 打开 系统设置 → 隐私与安全性。
  3. 往下滚动到"安全性"部分，会看到 DMS 被阻止的提示，点击旁边的"仍要打开"。
  4. 输入你的 Mac 密码或触控 ID 确认。
  5. 再次双击 DMS.app，这次会出现新的确认框，点击"打开"即可，以后不会再提示。

Windows：提示"Windows 已保护你的电脑"

  1. 点击提示框里的"更多信息"。
  2. 点击出现的"仍要运行"按钮。

Mac: "Apple could not verify... is free of malware"

  1. When you see this, click Done (not "Move to Trash").
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. Scroll down to the Security section — you'll see DMS listed as blocked. Click "Open Anyway" next to it.
  4. Enter your Mac password or Touch ID to confirm.
  5. Double-click DMS.app again — a new confirmation dialog appears, click Open. It won't ask again after this.

Windows: "Windows protected your PC"

  1. Click "More info" in the dialog.
  2. Click the "Run anyway" button that appears.
